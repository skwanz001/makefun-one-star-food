One sentence, at most 200 characters, in the voice of an alien tourist giving planet Earth a one-star review while still slipping in a real compliment about the food — entered in make.fun's "One Star, Five Stars for the Food" bounty.

## The sentence

> One star: I flew 40 light-years to a planet where gravity flattened my antennae, the air tastes of exhaust, and humans queue for everything, but the dumplings were so good I rebooked anyway.

190 characters, one sentence. The complaint is specific and alien (a 40-light-year trip, flattened antennae, exhaust air, endless queuing); the turn is the food — dumplings good enough to rebook a one-star planet.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` and verifies it round-trips exactly, within the 1–200 character gate, as a single sentence.
